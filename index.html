Buyer Protocol Guide

How to Acclimatize Your New Pullets & Hens

Follow our veterinarian-recommended 48-hour protocol upon uncrating your chickens to ensure rapid adaptation and seamless egg production start:

  • Provide fresh water infused with Electrolytes & Vitamin C immediately.
  • Keep in a draft-free quarantine coop for 3 days before mixing with existing flocks.
  • Feed 16%-18% Layer Mash for 5-month pullets to stimulate ovulation.
